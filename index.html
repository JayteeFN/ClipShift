About ClipShift

ClipShift is a curated home for automotive video culture: exhaust notes, turbo sounds, builds, cold starts, and meets. This early version uses example listings so you can test the experience before adding real videos.

To suggest a video, use the Submit a clip button. Submissions open your email app for review; they do not automatically publish.

Only submit videos you own or are authorized to share. Creators retain ownership of their content. For removal requests, use the contact address configured in the website file.

Privacy: Saved clips stay in your browser's local storage on this device. No user account or analytics are included in this demo.
